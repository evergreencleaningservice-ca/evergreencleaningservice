### Search Intent

Transactional-commercial. The searcher is a facility, office or property manager who wants a contractor for recurring cleaning and is comparing two or three. Google rewards a **service landing page** with scope lists, scheduling, trust proof and a quote form; informational "what is janitorial" content ranks only as a supporting section or FAQ, not as the page. Knowledge level: practical buyer who knows the term "janitorial" means routine, contract-based upkeep, and who will ask "what is the difference from commercial/office cleaning?", "what does it include?", "how is it priced?" and "are you insured/WSIB?".

### Content Gaps and Opportunities

**Topic gaps (nobody covers)**
- A plain **janitorial vs commercial vs office cleaning vs day porter** explainer that routes the reader to the right Evergreen page. Pro-Clean has one FAQ; nobody has a section with a comparison table.
- **Who supplies what**: consumables (paper, soap, liners), equipment, closet space, keys and alarm codes. Only the Evergreen office page mentions consumables at all.
- **Multi-site coordination for one GTA business** (head office + branch + warehouse under one contract and one contact). Generations has one FAQ line.
- A **visit frequency guide** by facility type (what nightly vs 3x week vs weekly usually means in practice).

**Depth gaps (covered but shallow)**
- "What's included" is a list of nouns everywhere. Nobody splits it into *every visit* / *weekly* / *periodic*, which is exactly what a written proposal does. Evergreen's office page already has the every-visit / periodic split; reuse the structure.
- Process: competitors say "walkthrough, proposal, start". None says what happens at the walkthrough (measure, count washrooms, note floor types, agree access).
- Pricing: only Pro-Clean lists factors. Nobody gives a worked explanation of why janitorial is quoted monthly and what drives it.

**Quality gaps**
- JAN-PRO, Jani-King and Coverall are franchise pages that cannot name a Toronto crew or office. Evergreen can: one Toronto office since 1989, crews dispatched from it (keep the wording the site already uses; no "local crews in every city").
- We Clean It and Cleantegra have no FAQ; Jani-King has no alt text; Cleantegra leads with a discount.

**Gap priority (Impact × Advantage ÷ Effort):**
1. Janitorial-vs-other-services comparison table with internal links — high impact, unique, low effort.
2. Every-visit / weekly / periodic scope table — high impact, moderate effort (reuse office page structure).
3. "Who supplies what" and access section — medium impact, unique, low effort.
4. Pricing factors with monthly-contract framing — medium impact, low effort (template already has pricing factors).
5. Multi-site section — medium impact, needs client confirmation of how they actually handle it.
