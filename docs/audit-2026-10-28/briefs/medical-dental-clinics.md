### Content Gaps and Opportunities (what to add or strengthen)

**Topic gaps — add**
- **Ours vs yours: a responsibilities table.** Environmental surfaces, floors, washrooms, waiting rooms, staff rooms are ours; clinical contact surfaces between patients, dental unit waterlines, instrument reprocessing, sterilizer, sharps and biomedical waste are the clinic's under its IPAC protocol. Pro-Clean has a paragraph; nobody has a table. SKYREX gets it wrong, which makes a correct table more valuable.
- **Standards named correctly for Ontario**: RCDSO *Standard of Practice: Infection Prevention and Control in the Dental Office*; Public Health Ontario / PIDAC *Best Practices for Environmental Cleaning for Prevention and Control of Infections in All Health Care Settings*; disinfectants with a Health Canada DIN and the labelled contact time. Link to each. Nobody in the Toronto set cites them by name.
- **End-of-day order of work**: operatories first while surfaces are dry, then corridors, washrooms last; separate colour-coded cloths and mop heads for washrooms vs clinical rooms; no re-entry to cleaned operatories. Toronto Dental Office Cleaning Services mentions "end-of-visit checks"; nobody explains the sequence or why.
- **Medical clinics beyond dental**: family practice, physio, optometry, labs — the current page lists them but says nothing specific (exam-table paper, specimen areas not touched, waiting-room toys). One short subsection.

**Depth gaps — strengthen**
- Frequency: add a room / every visit / weekly / periodic table (SKYREX has daily/weekly/monthly; Toronto Dental Office Cleaning Services has 1–3 / 5 / 7 days per week). Put both ideas in one table.
- Products: the page names ProSeries Green for cleaning (pending A5) but says only "specialized products" for disinfection. Name the disinfectant class, confirm it carries a DIN, and state contact time is followed — after the client confirms what they use.
- FAQ: four is thin; add four (see outline).
- Pricing: factors exist; add the clinic-specific drivers (number of operatories, washrooms, days per week, whether daily disinfection of waiting rooms is included).

**Quality gaps — fix**
- Proof: no clinic review, no case. Ask the client whether a dental or clinic client will give a short quote (A11/B3 process); the Mississauga client copy referenced in the inventory may name a clinic.
- Images: two, one of a dental chair that may be generated (B5).
- The "specialists vs typical companies" comparison on the exact-match-domain competitor is effective; Evergreen can do a sober version ("what changes when the office is a clinic") without disparaging anyone.

**Gap priority:**
1. Responsibilities table (ours / clinic's) with standards links — highest impact, unique, low effort, protects the client.
2. End-of-day sequence and cross-contamination controls — high impact, unique, needs one confirmation of practice.
3. Room / frequency table — high impact, low effort.
4. Four new FAQs — medium, low effort.
5. Clinic review and real photo — high impact, blocked on client.
