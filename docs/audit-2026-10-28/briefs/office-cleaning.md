### Search Intent

Transactional. Office managers, operations leads and small-business owners in Toronto looking for a recurring cleaner, usually to replace one. Google rewards a **service landing page with scope, scheduling, proof and a quote path**; informational content ranks only as FAQ. The audience knows what they want cleaned and is comparing on trust (insurance, screening, consistency), convenience (after hours, one contact) and price certainty.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities (what to add or strengthen)

**Topic gaps — add**
- **A downloadable or on-page office cleaning checklist** by area (reception, workstations, boardrooms, kitchens, washrooms, floors) and frequency. We Clean It has "routine vs deep add-ons"; nobody publishes a usable checklist. The site already has blog material (/key-office-areas-to-have-cleaned-and-disinfected/, /areas-in-your-office-where-germs-are-likely-hiding/) to draw from.
- **Switching providers**: how a handover works (keys, alarm codes, supply closet, first deep clean, 30-day review). The commonest real situation; nobody addresses it. Requires confirming the practice (B1-adjacent).
- **Day or night?** A short section (the site has a blog post on this) that links to day porter coverage once that page exists.
- **Toronto specifics**: Financial District towers with loading-dock and freight-elevator rules, after-hours security sign-in, winter entrance mats — the things that make a Toronto office different from a suburban one. Coverall gestures at "Financial and Professional Sectors" without substance.

**Depth gaps — strengthen**
- **Proof.** Two Google reviews from "5 years ago" is the weakest element. Add the live GBP rating and count (B3), newer reviews if any, and a named client case once B4 produces one. Pro-Clean's founder bio and review markup are its edge; Evergreen can answer with "since 1989, one Toronto office, same owner-operator" if the client confirms the founder story (about-us page).
- **Quality control.** The page says "inspections and adjustments" generically. Competitors (JAN-PRO Tracker, Cleantegra "regular on-site inspection") name a routine. Add the actual routine and frequency once B1 is supplied; until then leave as is.
- **Pricing.** Factors are good; add one paragraph on how office cleaning is quoted (monthly fixed price for an agreed scope; what changes it; the walkthrough) and the honest statement that per-square-foot or per-hour numbers given over the phone are guesses. Numbers only if A7 is confirmed for office contracts.
- **FAQ.** Seven is good; add three: "What is the difference between office cleaning and janitorial services?", "Do you supply paper towels, soap and liners?", "What happens if something is missed?" (answer only with B2).

**Quality gaps — fix**
- Images: three images, at least one reads as generated (B5). Replace with real crew photos; the alt text is already descriptive.
- Reviews: show source and date, as now, but refresh.
- The two legacy paragraphs are fine; tighten "friendly handshake" if the client agrees, but do not remove the client's voice.

**Gap priority:**
1. Proof refresh (GBP rating, newer reviews, real photos) — highest impact, blocked on B3/B5.
2. On-page checklist by area and frequency — high impact, low effort, unique.
3. Switching-providers / handover section — high impact, unique, needs one client confirmation.
