## 2. Content gaps vs what currently ranks

1. **No Canadian/Toronto voice in the "franchise vs local" SERP.** Every ranking page is US/Australian franchisor content. Ontario specifics — WSIB clearance certificates, WHMIS, who holds the account when the cleaner is a unit franchisee — are absent.
2. **Nobody explains the three-tier structure in buyer terms.** JAN-PRO's own "How JAN-PRO Canada works" page does so for prospective franchisees; no third party translates it into "who is in my building and whose insurance covers them."
3. **No sourced feature matrix.** Ranking pages are prose; a dated, linked side-by-side (ownership, contact, WSIB, guarantee, pricing floor, locations) is a format gap.
4. **No "questions to ask" list with Ontario documents named.** The one result that names WSIB is a US business outlet.
5. **Pricing honesty gap.** JAN-PRO Canada publishes a from-price ($250/month); most local firms publish nothing. Evergreen can at least publish its price drivers (already on `/services/office-cleaning/`) and, if the owner decides, a public from-price with conditions.
6. **Guarantee gap (Evergreen's own).** JAN-PRO publishes a written guarantee; Evergreen does not. This is a product gap the page will expose — fix it before launch (RECOMMENDATIONS.md §4).

## 4. Can an "alternatives"/"vs" page from a one-office local company rank?

- For **Group A (franchise vs local)**: realistically yes, over time. The incumbents are low-authority regional franchise blogs; the format gap is real; the query is informational so a .ca domain with a sourced table and Toronto specifics can rank in Canada without brand authority.
- For **Group B/C (brand terms)**: no. Brand pages, GBP and review platforms hold the top; a competitor's page would sit far below and carry trademark/affiliation risk. The sensible way to capture that intent is FAQ 1 on the Group A page and, if the owner wants, a low-budget PPC test (RECOMMENDATIONS.md §6).
- For **Group D (best/office cleaning companies toronto)**: directories dominate; Evergreen's best route is being listed and reviewed on them plus strengthening its own service and location pages.
