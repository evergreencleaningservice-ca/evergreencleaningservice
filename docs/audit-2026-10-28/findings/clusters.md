## 0. Data caveats (read first)

1. **Not geo-personalised.** The WebSearch tool runs from the US and does not return Toronto-local results or a local pack. I compensated with "toronto", "gta" and "mississauga" modifiers, but real Google.ca rankings will differ.
2. **Not a Google index.** The tool's results are heavy on directories (HomeStars, Yabsta, RenoQuotes), job boards and spam pages (Notion, Pearltrees, yolasite, gumroad). As a result, overlap counts here are lower and noisier than a Google SERP would give. The *relative* pattern still holds: commercial/office/janitorial terms share URLs, and specialty terms share none. Re-run the 10 head terms in DataForSEO or GSC before committing budget to a borderline split.
3. **No search-volume data** (no DataForSEO access). Head terms and the pillar were chosen by breadth and by how many variants share their SERP, not by volume.
4. **Evergreen appeared in 0 of the 42 top-10 sets.** JAN-PRO appeared in top 10 for 3 terms (retail cleaning, how to choose, commercial vs janitorial) and in top 20 for 4 more. It does not appear for any Toronto-modified transactional term in this index.
5. The blog-post fold/redirect advice is based on slug, topic and on-site linking, not on traffic. **Check GSC clicks and backlinks before any 301:** keep any post with real clicks or links and merge into it instead.

## 8. Priority reasoning for a one-office Toronto business

- **Depth beats breadth.** JAN-PRO runs 25 industry pages and about 15 service pages because hundreds of franchisees fund them. A single-office firm should copy the *structure* (one URL per service with its own SERP) only where the overlap data shows a distinct SERP **and** Evergreen already does the work. That is why strip & wax, carpet, day porter and windows come before more industry pages.
- **Go where competitors are weak.** For strip & wax, day porter, windows and gyms, the observed Toronto-modified SERPs are filled with job ads, US franchise pages or spam. One solid local page with real photos, a crew description and a walkthrough offer can rank there. Head terms like "commercial cleaning toronto" are contested by Coverall and Enviro-Master, so the pillar wins through internal-link mass and depth rather than on its own.
- **Revenue fit.** Floor care, carpet and day porter are add-ons to existing recurring office contracts and to the property-management segment Evergreen already targets. They raise contract value without new crews or new geography.
- **Bottom-funnel content first, informational last.** The cost guide and buyer's guide come before checklists because they convert. Blog consolidation (#10 and section 5) is low effort and stops 39 thin posts from diluting crawl budget and topical signals.
- **Stay within the 7 GTA locations.** Don't add more city pages until the service set is complete. K24/K25 show that location pages are justified (4 overlap within Mississauga, 0 with Toronto), but each one needs unique proof (clients, buildings served) to avoid doorway-page risk.
