## Blog photo captions

- Keep captions that help readers understand the photo or apply the advice in the article.
- Omit generic stock-photo notices such as "Illustrative stock photograph." and visible Magnific credits on blog photos whose licence permits omission. Keep the source and licence details in the internal photo records.
- Before publishing or editing a blog post, review the rendered captions for stock-photo notices and Magnific credits. Remove the notice or credit where permitted. Remove the caption entirely if it contains only a notice or credit.
