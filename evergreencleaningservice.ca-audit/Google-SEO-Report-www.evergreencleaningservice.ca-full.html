1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of www.evergreencleaningservice.ca for a Local Service (hybrid): commercial and office cleaning company, one office at 243 Queen St W, Toronto, serving Toronto and 9 GTA cities site, generated on November 06, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

76/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. Mobile homepage Lighthouse performance 51: Google tag scripts (gtm.js 157 KB + gtag.js 194 KB) block the main thread for 1,345 ms and inflate the simulated LCP to 6.8 s (observed LCP 1.5 s)
  2. No named person anywhere on the site: no owner, account executive or author; all 25 posts are credited to the organization and the About page names nobody
  3. The office cleaning cost guide (title '2026 Pricing Guide') gives only figures it labels hypothetical, so it fails the cost intent and gives AI engines nothing to quote
  4. Third-party proof is thin: 7 Google reviews (4 of them 5-6 years old), no case study published, all imagery stock, no citations found under the current NAP on YellowPages or BBB; social profile URLs still carry the former name
  5. Three URLs compete for 'commercial cleaning Toronto' (homepage, /services/commercial-cleaning/, /locations/toronto-downtown/), reinforced by sitewide footer anchors
Quick Wins:
  1. Defer the GTM loader to first interaction or idle, with tel:/form-focus triggers (src/components/GoogleTagManager.astro)
  2. Stop the italic Open Sans face loading on the homepage (.tes-name normal; scope the face to post pages): -50 KB in the LCP window
  3. Fix the sitewide footer anchors in src/data/site.ts: 'Toronto' -> 'Downtown Toronto', repoint or remove 'Toronto & GTA', make Richmond Hill and Scarborough plain text until pages exist
  4. Retitle the cost guide ('How Office Cleaning Is Priced in Toronto') or publish real price bands from recent proposals
  5. Add the brand suffix to the 22 bare blog titles, trim 8 meta descriptions over 160 chars and the 64-char commercial title

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 86/100

What Works

  • All 92 crawled URLs return 200; no broken internal links; one H1, lang=en-CA, viewport and self-canonical on every indexable page
  • http->https, apex->www and slashless->slashed are all 301; /index.html 307; legacy WordPress paths (wp-login, xmlrpc, feed, sitemap_index.xml) are 404 or 301 correctly; real 404 page with noindex
  • Static HTML: content, JSON-LD and hero image present without JavaScript; Brotli on HTML and assets; /_astro/* immutable 1 year; HTML at Cloudflare edge
  • Security headers on every response: HSTS 1y, nosniff, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options, Permissions-Policy, CSP (report-only)
  • Sitemaps valid, split by type, 57/57 URLs 200 + indexable + self-canonical; no noindex URL listed; lastmod from git history, not build time

Findings

CSP is report-only with unsafe-inline and unsafe-eval Medium

content-security-policy-report-only on every response; Lighthouse csp-xss reports no enforced CSP. Hardening, not a ranking factor.

Recommendation: Enforce the non-script directives now (frame-ancestors, object-src, base-uri, form-action) in public/_headers; drop unsafe-eval after the GTM container is audited.

robots.txt Content-Signal lines flagged by Lighthouse as unknown directives Low

Lighthouse SEO scores 92 on every page because of the two Content-Signal lines. Googlebot ignores unknown fields; crawl is unaffected.

Recommendation: Owner decision: keep (and document the expected Lighthouse flag) or remove, since allow-all is already the default.

17 sitemap lastmod values share one template-commit timestamp Low

services/*, industries/* and locations/* all show 2026-10-31T14:11:02Z from a shared quote-form commit, not a content change.

Recommendation: Exclude shared templates (OfferPage.astro, locations/[slug].astro) from the date sources in src/lib/sitemap-lastmod.mjs; add a CI check that every <url> has a <lastmod>.

Plain http:// internal link on /privacy/ Low

Only http internal link in the crawl; adds a redirect hop and created a duplicate crawl entry.

Recommendation: Change the href to https://www.evergreencleaningservice.ca/ in the privacy page source.

HTML Content-Type has no charset Low

content-type: text/html with no charset; <meta charset> saves browsers, but naive fetchers decode em dashes as mojibake.

Recommendation: Add 'Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8' for /* in public/_headers (and text/plain; charset=utf-8 for /llms.txt).

Two-hop redirect for http/apex URLs without trailing slash Low

http://evergreencleaningservice.ca/about-us -> https://www.../about-us -> /about-us/.

Recommendation: Append the slash in the Cloudflare host redirect rule, or accept.

Third-party cookie from DoubleClick (best-practices 79) Low

test_cookie set by the Google Ads view-through tag.

Recommendation: Gate remarketing behind consent mode or disable view-through if unused; otherwise accept.

25 noindex tag/category archives linked from every post Info

follow,noindex archives receive internal links; crawl budget is not a concern at 92 URLs.

Recommendation: No change needed.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 68/100

What Works

  • Service and industry pages are substantive (667-2,435 words), specific about scope, scheduling and limits, with FAQ blocks; readability Flesch ~57-64, grade 8-9
  • Location pages are not city-swaps in the body: 2-12% verbatim overlap, real districts, corridors and drive times, honest 'no office in X' statements
  • Blog update dates match real rewrites in git; no templated metadata; plugin content-quality scan clean (no filler or AI-pattern flags)
  • Page types match search intent on all 8 core queries (no page-type mismatch)

Findings

No named person anywhere on the site High

About page names nobody; all 25 BlogPosting authors are the Organization; the only personal name is 'Art' inside a 2021 review. Repo docs list this as waiting on the client (H5).

Recommendation: Add owner and account executive(s) with photo, role and years to /about-us/; Person nodes (founder/employee) in src/lib/schema.ts; a reviewer line on the 2026 guides.

Cost guide does not answer its own question High

/office-cleaning-cost-toronto-guide/ is titled '2026 Pricing Guide' but every figure is labelled hypothetical and 'not Evergreen quotes'. Competing results (HomeStars $30-60/hr) give numbers.

Recommendation: Publish Evergreen's real bands by office size and frequency from recent proposals (needs client decision D-D), or retitle to 'How Office Cleaning Is Priced in Toronto'.

All imagery is stock; no case study is published; gallery is 71 words High

13 gallery photos are stock with job-like filenames; the one real project (warehouse strip and wax, ~50k sq ft) is a draft held by the photo-approval gate.

Recommendation: Run the photoshoot (H6); publish 2-3 case studies via src/content/projects; until then noindex /cleaning-demo-gallery/ or caption it honestly.

Trust claims asserted but never evidenced Medium

'WSIB covered, bonded and insured' on 24-25 pages and 'since 1989' on 26, with no certificate, insurer, coverage amount or dated artefact.

Recommendation: Add a proof block on /about-us/ and the quote page: 'Certificate of insurance and WSIB clearance available on request', insurer and coverage, screening method.

Keyword cannibalisation on 'commercial cleaning Toronto' Medium

Homepage, /services/commercial-cleaning/ and /locations/toronto-downtown/ all target it; footer anchors on 60 pages send 'Toronto' to the downtown page and 'Toronto & GTA' to the commercial page; 'Richmond Hill' and 'Scarborough' anchors point at the bare hub.

Recommendation: One primary query per URL; in src/data/site.ts footerAreas rename 'Toronto' to 'Downtown Toronto', repoint or remove 'Toronto & GTA', make the two city anchors plain text.

Location pages share one heading outline; Mississauga and Vaughan intros switch to generic sales voice Medium

5 of 7 pages open with the same sentence; 'top-tier', 'Zero Disruption', 'Total peace of mind' copy in src/data/locations.ts.

Recommendation: Rewrite the two intros in the house voice; give each page a unique local opening; vary H2s where content allows.

15 legacy posts untouched since 2019-2023, with unsourced statistics and defects Medium

'5 Easy Ways' post says 'our top three tips'; plants, deep-cleaning and slips posts cite 'studies' without sources; 13 posts have no outbound citation.

Recommendation: Fix the defects; cite or delete statistics; update or consolidate in priority order (dental, retail, day vs night, deep cleaning, dusting); consider noindexing plants, home-office and fridge posts.

Quote form too long for a first price question Medium

About 9 required fields including full street address before any price; the SERP persona wanting a ballpark scores 45/100.

Recommendation: Make street address optional, keep postal code, add sq ft / visits per week / washrooms, offer a ballpark-by-email path (client decision D-A).

Thin utility pages and manufacturer copy Medium

/contact-us/ 122 words, /request-a-quote/ 158, /locations/ 229, /green-clean-products/ mostly manufacturer copy with a 2013 news line.

Recommendation: Add 'what happens next' and a trust strip to contact and quote pages; rewrite green-clean-products in house words.

Persona-matched reviews not shown on the pages that need them Medium

Clinic, floor-stripping and post-construction pages show no testimonial; the only property-manager review sits on Mississauga and commercial-cleaning.

Recommendation: Tag reviews by service in src/data/reviews.ts and render the matching one per page; date or drop the three undated homepage testimonials.

Service pages carry 18-44% shared boilerplate; building-maintenance and floor-stripping overlap Low

Trust band, 'How it works', 'Where we work' repeat verbatim.

Recommendation: Shorten 'How it works' to a link to /our-process/ on specialty pages; make building-maintenance the hub and link to floor-stripping for detail.

Footer emits five H2s on every page; homepage uses headings as labels Low

Dilutes the heading outline without breaking it.

Recommendation: Change footer titles to styled <p>; demote homepage label headings.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 82/100

What Works

  • Coherent JSON-LD graph on all pages: ProfessionalService (#organization) with address, geo, hours, areaServed, sameAs, contactPoint, offer catalog; WebSite; BreadcrumbList valid on 90 pages; Service + WebPage on 19; BlogPosting complete on 25; FAQPage text matches visible text on all 78 Q&As
  • No parse errors, no duplicate @ids, no deprecated types; aggregateRating correctly omitted (self-serving reviews earn no stars)

Findings

WebSite SearchAction points at a noindex, JS-only search page Low

Sitelinks search box was retired by Google in Nov 2024; /search/ is noindex and covers blog posts only.

Recommendation: Remove potentialAction from the WebSite node in src/lib/schema.ts.

logo and image are the same 884x265 banner Low

Google prefers a square-croppable logo and real photos for image.

Recommendation: Supply a 512x512 logo for logo and 1-2 real photos as an image array (after the photoshoot).

BlogPosting image is 2:1 (960x480) on 23 posts; dates at midnight UTC Low

Google recommends 16:9, 4:3, 1:1; the 1200x630 OG images exist.

Recommendation: Pass [ogImage, featureImage] to image in articleSchema(); emit date-only or offset-aware dates.

FAQPage markup earns no rich result since May 2026 Info

Accurate and harmless; the visible FAQs are the value.

Recommendation: Keep the visible FAQs; do not add more FAQPage markup; keeping or removing the JSON-LD is low stakes.

Breadcrumb root name differs on two posts ('Home' vs site name); location Service nodes thinner than service-page ones; streetAddress has trailing period Low

Cosmetic consistency issues.

Recommendation: Use site.name for the root in the post breadcrumb builder; have the location template call serviceSchema(); drop the period if the Google listing has none.

2.5 Performance (CWV)

Score: 62/100

What Works

  • CLS 0 on every mobile run; hero image has dimensions, fetchpriority=high, AVIF/WebP sources, preconnect to the image host; only slide 1 loads eagerly
  • All CSS inlined (zero render-blocking requests); site JS ~9 KB; reCAPTCHA loaded on form intent only; video widget preload=none
  • Caching correct: /_astro immutable, images 30d/1y edge, HTML revalidated at the edge; Brotli; HTTP/2 and h3

Findings

Google tag scripts drive TBT and the lost mobile score High

Third-party code blocks the main thread for 1,345 ms on the mobile homepage; gtag/js alone has a 962 ms long task. Inner pages still pay ~500 ms. There is exactly one gtm.js and one gtag.js, so no duplicate to remove.

Recommendation: Defer the GTM loader to first interaction or idle (~4 s), with immediate triggers on tel: pointerdown and form focus; container hygiene (one Google tag for AW + GA4, prune unused tags). Monitor Ads/GA4 for a week after shipping. Not recommended: Partytown, server-side tagging as a first step.

Simulated LCP 6.8 s vs observed 1.5 s on the mobile homepage Medium

Lantern models ~330 KB of contending bytes (two 50 KB fonts, logo, hero, gtm.js) at 1.6 Mbps. The hero AVIF is 14.8 KB and loaded in 786 ms.

Recommendation: Remove the competing bytes (defer GTM, drop the italic font from the homepage); optional mobile hero preload; re-measure and add real-user monitoring.

Italic Open Sans (50 KB) fetched on the homepage for the testimonial <cite> only Medium

Requested at VeryHigh priority during the hero download; the only CSS italic rule is for blog blockquotes.

Recommendation: Set .tes-name { font-style: normal } and scope the italic @font-face to post pages.

INP unmeasured; risk from long third-party tasks and the reCAPTCHA burst on first form touch Medium

Eight long tasks up to 962 ms on the homepage trace; ~700 KB of reCAPTCHA scripting loads on first interaction with the quote form.

Recommendation: Add web-vitals RUM; consider Turnstile (already supported in the Worker) or earlier idle loading of the captcha near the form.

Service-card images oversized; Cloudflare Insights beacon on every page Low

sizes='100vw' for a 380 px card picks the 768 variant (~25-30 KB waste across three cards); beacon.min.js (10 KB) is injected at the edge.

Recommendation: Fix sizes and add a 640w variant; switch off Web Analytics auto-inject if GA4 is the system of record.
