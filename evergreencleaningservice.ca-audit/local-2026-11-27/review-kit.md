# Google review kit (27 Nov 2026)

**Goal:** fresh Google reviews from current clients. The site shows 7 reviews, and 4 of them are from 2020–21. Review count and recency are among the strongest signals for the map results that appear above everything else for "office cleaning Toronto".

## The link to send

**https://www.evergreencleaningservice.ca/review/**

- It opens Google's "write a review" box for the Evergreen Office Cleaning listing directly.
- Use it in emails, texts, invoices, email signatures and a printed card.
- It works once the site change that adds it is deployed.
- The direct Google link, in case it's ever needed: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ-1EamtnN1IkRb1yrFD38h9g

## Who to ask, and when

1. **Start with the 10 happiest current clients:** long contracts, recent compliments, no open issues. Ask the person who deals with the account executive, not a generic inbox.
2. **Then ask every new client** about 30 days after the first clean, once the crew has settled.
3. **Good moments to ask:**
   - after a passed inspection;
   - after a thank-you email;
   - after a one-off job such as an emergency clean-up or a post-construction clean that went well.
4. **Aim for 2–4 new reviews a month.** A steady trickle looks natural and keeps the profile fresh. A burst of 20 in a week, then nothing, does not.

## Google's rules (breaking them can get reviews removed)

- Ask everyone you'd normally ask. Don't first screen for happy clients with a "were you satisfied?" step.
- Never offer a discount, gift or credit for a review.
- Never write reviews for clients, or post them from staff or family accounts.
- Reply to every review, good or bad, within a few days.

## Replying to reviews (templates)

**Positive:**

> Thank you, [Name]! It's great to hear the [office / clinic / building] is in good shape. We'll pass this on to [crew lead / the crew]. — Evergreen Office Cleaning

**Critical:**

> Thank you for telling us, [Name]. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to. [Account executive] will call you today to put it right. You can also reach us at (416) 803-4880. — Evergreen Office Cleaning

Write each reply fresh. Name the specifics, and don't paste the same text every time.
