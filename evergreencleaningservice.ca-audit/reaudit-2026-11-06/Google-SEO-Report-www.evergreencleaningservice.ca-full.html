Recommendation: Read /api/csp-report and add the hosts before enforcing (~12 Nov)

Content-Signal lines flagged by Lighthouse Low

SEO 92 instead of 100

Recommendation: Owner decision: keep and document, or remove

Two-hop http/apex redirect without trailing slash Low

http://evergreencleaningservice.ca/about-us -> https://www/about-us -> /about-us/

Recommendation: Add the slash in the Cloudflare host redirect, or accept
