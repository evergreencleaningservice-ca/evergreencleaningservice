## Retail Storefront Checklist:

– Vacuum all of the carpets

– Sweep and mop all of the hardwood floors

– Empty all of the trash cans

– Get the dust off shelving units, countertops, and fixtures

– Sanitize everything from the counters to the door handles

## Retail Store Office Checklist:

– Vacuum all of the carpets

– Sweep and/or mop the hardwood floors

– Get rid of the trash out of the trashcans

![retail outlet](/images/retail-outlet.jpg)

## Retail Store Break room Cleaning Checklist:

– Vacuum all of the carpets

– Sweep and/or mop all of the hardwood floors

– Empty all of the trashcans

– Sanitize and wipe down all of the microwaves, coffee equipment, and countertops

– Change out the paper towels

## Retail Store Bathroom Cleaning Checklist:

– Vacuum all of the carpets

– Empty the trashcans

– Restock everything including hand soap, paper towels, and toilet paper

– Clean all of the toilets and urinals

– Clean the sinks

## Retail Storefront Exterior Cleaning Checklist:

– Empty all of the trashcans

– Clean the windows and the glass panes

– Sanitize the door handles

– Sweep the walkway
