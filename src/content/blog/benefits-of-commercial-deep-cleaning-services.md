## Why a Building Needs a Deep Clean

A clean workplace matters to the health and comfort of your employees and to how the building reads to clients and partners. A superficial tidy might seem acceptable here and there, but that’s not enough to achieve a degree of cleanliness that ascertains optimum operation. Ultimately, dust will gather, bacteria will multiply, and your workplace will become a demotivating space for your employees and unappealing for your clients and partners. In addition, you can only put off certain deep cleaning tasks for so long. Whether your premises is a warehouse, retail space, or office complex, commercial cleaning services are essential. If you are still on the fence or hesitant, here are some great benefits of hiring commercial cleaners.

### First Impressions are Important

No matter the industry, having a pristine space to welcome both clients and partners is vital for first impressions. When you walk into business premises, you want to feel comfortable, safe, and welcomed. Also, cleanliness and organization show that you are serious with your operations and can be trusted. Availing of regular deep cleaning services goes a long way to make sure potential or returning clients feel a sense of security and know that they can rely on your business.

### Motivate Your Employees

Ever had a dirty or cluttered living room or bedroom and just couldn’t relax? The same applies to workplaces. Your employees matter just as much as your customers. Allergens that settle on carpets, curtains, and upholstery affect the office’s air quality resulting in discomfort and health issues. As mentioned earlier, employees are bound to be more motivated and productive when they work in a clean and organized environment. This is especially the case when they do not have to do the cleaning themselves.

### Prevent Germs from Spreading

Certain bacteria and viruses can thrive on surfaces like tables, doorknobs, light switches, countertops, etc, resulting in the spread of diseases. To keep your team members healthy and productive, reduce the number of sick days and protect your clients and partners from diseases, it is important to ensure the workplace is clean and disinfected. The only way to ensure this is through [regular cleaning](/services/office-cleaning/) and [periodic deep cleaning](/services/building-maintenance/), and this is where seasoned commercial cleaners come in handy.

### When You Need One

Routine cleaning keeps a building presentable; a deep clean resets it. The usual occasions are after a renovation, a move or a long period of heavy use; before a reopening, an inspection or an important visit; when carpets, grout or high surfaces have stopped responding to routine cleaning; and at a quiet point in the year, such as the end of summer or the holidays, when fewer people are in the way. Many buildings book one or two a year alongside their regular schedule.
