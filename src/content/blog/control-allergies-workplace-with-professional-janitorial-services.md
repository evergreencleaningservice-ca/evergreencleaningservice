Allergies are undoubtedly one of the most irritating things in the workplace that can affect both the efficiency and productivity of the employees. Allergies take a toll on the human body and prevent it from performing certain tasks. The health of an employees is paramount for any company out there as it contributes to a great working environment. This translates to more productivity and, ultimately, more revenue.

That’s why some of the most successful companies in the world put extra effort to ascertain a healthy work environment for their employees. Ask any seasoned manager or business owner, and they will explain why it’s essential to maintain a healthy workplace.

![Cleaner vacuuming an open-plan office with a backpack vacuum](/images/office-backpack-vacuum-toronto.jpg)

This is one of the primary reasons why businesses use [janitorial services](/services/). These services help control allergies by ensuring that no allergens are present in the workplace. There are many common allergens that a professional janitorial service will eliminate.

The first stage of ensuring that your workplace is free of these allergens is asking the workforce whether they have any allergies. This will give you an idea of the allergens that need to be eliminated. Ideally, it will be easy to identify people in your workforce with unusual allergies. All this information will come in handy when briefing the janitorial service provider on your needs and requirements.

So let’s further discuss how professional janitorial services can be helpful in controlling allergens in your office.

### Hard Surface Cleaners

Hard surfaces are notorious for harboring allergies. These surfaces are hard to clean, and this makes it easy for allergens to make them home for a long time. This exposes you and your team to the risk of getting allergies.

This is where hard surface cleaners come into the picture. They ensure that all these surfaces are cleaned properly and that all the allergens are eliminated. This prevents substantial damage to the health of your employees. It is ideally imperative to know that this is a requirement for the productivity of your workforce. A clean workplace boosts the morale of the staff, which in turn improves their performance and productivity.

Making the employees feel safe is a primary responsibility of any business owner, and a safe workplace has higher chances of producing the best results. Since hard surfaces are a prominent source of allergens, then you should use hard surface cleaners to get rid of those allergies in the workplace.

### Chemical-Based Carpet Cleaners

Carpets are another source of allergens, mostly because the fabrics are great at trapping them. When the allergens are disturbed, usually by walking on the carpet, they disperse, and when they come into contact with an allergic person, their performance in the workplace is hampered. That’s why it is important to ensure that your office carpets are always clean and free of allergens.

There are numerous people or services that can help with carpet cleaning, but allergens removal calls for professionals. Getting rid of allergens from carpets is a tricky endeavor that calls for specific cleaning methods and products. As such, it is important to hire [professional carpet cleaners](/services/commercial-cleaning/).

Carpets are certainly the most apparent allergy sources due to their ability to trap allergens. The fabrics provide adequate shelter and food for allergens to survive for long. Carpets ideally call for thorough cleaning in order to ensure that the workplace is clean and appealing but also protect the staff. By hiring professional carpet cleaners, you make a huge step in boosting the efficiency and productivity of the workforce.

### Pet Dander & Odor

Pet danger allergies are another common form of allergies nowadays. They spread through pet hair and odor and can be quite disruptive for everyone in the workplace, not only making health concerns but also taking away the morale of the employees. Nobody wants to work in an office that’s full of pet odor and dander. Again, this is where professional janitorial services come in handy. They ensure that these allergens are eliminated through thorough cleaning, thus improving the air quality in the office.

### Dust and Pollen

Dust and pollen allergies are the most common and serious allergies today. They consist of small particles that propagate in air, making their way into the human body through breathing. Air is present everywhere, and so, these particles can travel virtually anywhere. This is what makes this allergy hard to deal with.

Pollen and dust allergies elimination should be a high priority if you want to maintain a healthy workplace. If someone gets an allergy within your office, chances are its dust or pollen.

Another thing that makes it hard to deal with dust and pollen is that the particles are too small to be visible by the naked eye. They propagate through the air, and so, no place is safe. As such, you’ll want to hire an experienced janitorial service provider to tackle the issue. They will clear the workplace of these allergens through professional cleaning. It is ideally advisable to install [air purifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_purifier) in the office as they help get rid of dust and pollen allergens.
