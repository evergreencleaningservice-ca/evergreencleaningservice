## Get Your Office Space Professionally Cleaned

With the summer vacation season over and fall being in full swing, there is no better time than now to get your office space professionally cleaned. Use this opportunity while things at the office are somewhat slower and it is less crowded to make the space as clean as possible for your employees and guests. With that being said, here are some of the most crucial areas you need to have cleaned within your office.

![Cleaner vacuuming an open-plan office with a backpack vacuum](/images/office-backpack-vacuum-toronto.jpg)

### Reception/Lobby

Besides this area being the first thing that people see when they walk into your office, it is also the one place that is most contaminated by visitors. To ensure optimal cleanliness and safeguard the health of everyone within your office, make sure that everything that the public frequently comes into contact with, including door handles, the front desk, magazines, etc., are properly cleaned and disinfected on a regular basis. Also, make sure to have the floor in this area professionally cleaned at least once every 3-4 months. Now is the perfect time to get this done.

### Washrooms

The bathrooms are among the dirtiest spots in any establishment. They house thousands of different kinds of bacteria. Therefore, it is very important to have the public and employee washroom facilities are always maintained clean and sanitized to ensure a safe and comfortable environment for everyone using these areas. While public washrooms need to be cleaned multiple times a day, now might be a good time to give them special attention and have them professionally deep cleaned.
