<h2 id="office-cleaning-pricing">How Much Does Office Cleaning Cost in Toronto?</h2>

There is no single price that fits every Toronto office. Two buildings with the same floor area can require different amounts of work: one may have open workstations and a small kitchenette, while the other has several washrooms, meeting rooms and a busy reception area. A useful quote starts with those details.

For an early budget, multiply an assumed cost per visit by the number of visits in a month. The table below demonstrates that calculation. **These are hypothetical CAD calculations before HST, not Evergreen quotes or verified GTA market averages.** The assumed $100–$300 per visit is an arithmetic input, not a statement of what your office should cost.

**Assumptions:** 52 service weeks divided by 12 months, with no holidays or missed visits deducted. Average monthly visits are rounded to two decimal places first: 4.33, 8.67, 13.00 and 21.67. Multiplying these by the hypothetical $100–$300 per visit produces the displayed ranges. Actual invoice schedules, rounding and holiday arrangements may differ.

These examples do not assign a price to a particular office size or scope. They also do not budget separately for consumables, an initial deep clean or specialist floor work. Ask each provider to identify those items and any applicable taxes in writing. [Ontario's HST rate is 13%](https://www.ontario.ca/document/harmonized-sales-tax-hst).

<h2 id="whats-included-in-office-cleaning">What's Included in Office Cleaning?</h2>

Routine [office cleaning](/services/office-cleaning/) can cover accessible workstation surfaces, kitchens, washrooms, floors and shared spaces. The contract should specify the tasks for your building rather than relying on the phrase “general cleaning.” A written schedule also distinguishes work done every visit from tasks done periodically.

At workstations, clarify whether desks need to be cleared and whether monitors, keyboards or other equipment are excluded. In kitchens, identify accessible counters, sinks, tables and appliance exteriors. Interior fridge and oven cleaning should be discussed separately. In washrooms, specify fixtures, touchpoints, floors, bins and the arrangement for refilling dispensers.

<h2 id="comparing-cleaning-quotes">How Can You Compare Office Cleaning Quotes and Control Costs?</h2>

The most useful comparison is between written scopes with the same rooms, tasks and schedule. A lower monthly amount may cover fewer visits, leave out supplies or omit periodic work. Ask each provider to price the same requirements so that the differences are visible.

### Compare written scopes of work

List every room and the tasks expected there. Record the frequency, the service window, any exclusions and how additional work is authorized. Ask whether the quoted amount includes equipment and cleaning products, and whether an initial clean is separate. Avoid agreeing to extra work without a written price or approval process.

### Check insurance, bonding and WSIB

Ask for current evidence of liability insurance, the provider's bonding arrangements and applicable WSIB coverage or clearance. Confirm the documents relate to the business carrying out the work. Ask how damage, access incidents and concerns about the service are reported and handled.

### Ask about staff consistency and inspections

Find out whether a regular crew will service your office, who covers absences and how replacement staff learn your requirements. Ask who checks the work, how inspections are recorded and who you contact when something needs attention. The inspection process should refer back to the scope you agreed to.

### Confirm access and consumables

Put key handling, alarm instructions and restricted areas in the access plan. Identify responsibility for parking or security charges. For washroom and kitchen consumables, agree who purchases supplies, who replenishes them and whether there are separate delivery or service charges.

### Avoid common contract mistakes

- Comparing the monthly total without matching the number of visits and included tasks.
- Assuming carpet extraction, floor refinishing or appliance interiors are included.
- Overlooking renewal dates, cancellation notice, price reviews and holiday arrangements.
- Leaving the process for approving extra work or resolving missed tasks undefined.
- Reducing washroom and kitchen attention without considering how the office is used.

To control costs, keep desks clear, arrange storage so floors can be reached and choose a schedule based on actual occupancy. Discuss which low-use areas can be cleaned less often while giving busy areas the attention they need. For more questions to ask a provider, read [How to Decide on a Cleaning Company](/how-to-decide-on-a-cleaning-company/).

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<details>
<summary>How much does office cleaning cost per square foot in Toronto?</summary>
<p>A square-foot rate is useful only when you know the service frequency and the work included. Dividing a monthly quote by floor area gives a monthly figure per square foot; dividing a single-visit quote gives a different measure. Ask which one a provider is using and compare the same scope and period. This guide does not establish a verified Toronto rate per square foot. Request a site-specific written quote instead of treating the budgeting examples as a rate card.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is daily cleaning more expensive than weekly cleaning?</summary>
<p>With the same assumed price per visit, more visits increase the monthly total, as the examples above show. Actual quotes may use different visit lengths or divide tasks differently across the week, so the change is not necessarily a simple multiple. Compare the total monthly amount, the number of visits and the work completed at each visit.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are cleaning supplies included in commercial cleaning rates?</summary>
<p>That depends on the written agreement. Ask whether cleaning chemicals and equipment are included, and check toilet paper, hand soap, paper towels and bin liners separately. Consumables may be supplied by your office or charged under a separate arrangement. Record both the purchasing and replenishment responsibilities so that an apparently inclusive quote does not leave a gap.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does after-hours office cleaning cost more?</summary>
<p>It depends on the provider's schedule and your building's access requirements. Evening access may fit the service plan, while security attendance, parking, restricted access windows or special arrangements may affect a quote. Ask for any related charges to be identified in writing. Do not assume that every after-hours visit carries a surcharge.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How often should a small office be professionally cleaned?</summary>
<p>Start with the number of people using it, visitor traffic, food preparation, washroom use and the condition of the space between visits. A lightly occupied office may need a different contractor schedule from a busy office of the same size. Agree on day-to-day housekeeping between visits, review the results and adjust the professional cleaning schedule when occupancy or usage changes.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are carpet cleaning and floor waxing included?</summary>
<p>Do not assume specialist floor work is included in routine maintenance. Vacuuming and suitable damp mopping are different tasks from carpet extraction or floor stripping and waxing. Ask for the method, areas, frequency, preparation requirements and price of any specialist work to be listed separately, or explicitly included in the contract.</p>
</details>
