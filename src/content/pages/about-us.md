Evergreen Office Cleaning is a commercial cleaning company based in Toronto. Since 1989 we have cleaned offices, medical clinics, dental offices, retail stores, managed buildings and industrial sites across Toronto and the GTA, from one office at 243 Queen St W. For many years the business was known as Evergreen Cleaning Service; the name changed, the company did not.

![Janitorial cart beside a commercial entrance](/images/gallery-office-cleaning-team.jpg)

## What we do

- [Office cleaning](/services/office-cleaning/): recurring after-hours cleaning of desks, kitchens, washrooms, floors and the touch points everyone shares.
- [Commercial cleaning and janitorial services](/services/commercial-cleaning/) for retail, institutional and mixed-use buildings.
- [Building maintenance and floor care](/services/building-maintenance/): [floor stripping and waxing](/services/floor-stripping-waxing/), carpet extraction, high dusting, glass and pressure washing.
- [Industrial and warehouse cleaning](/services/industrial-cleaning/), scheduled around shifts.
- [Post-construction cleaning](/services/post-construction-cleaning/), [disinfection cleaning](/services/disinfection-cleaning/) and [emergency clean-ups](/services/emergency-cleaning/).

## Who we clean for

Offices and professional firms, [medical and dental clinics](/industries/medical-dental-clinics/), [property managers and condominium buildings](/industries/property-management/), retail stores and showrooms, institutional and public buildings, and industrial buildings and warehouses.

## How we work

- **A reply within 2 hours.** Send the quote form or call, and an account executive gets back to you, not an auto-responder.
- **A free walkthrough.** When you are ready, we walk the building with you rather than pricing it over the phone.
- **A written scope.** The proposal sets out what is cleaned, how often and when, so nothing depends on someone remembering it.
- **The same crew every visit,** who learn your building and its rules.
- **One account executive,** who coordinates inspections, changes and extra work.
- **After hours and at weekends,** so your staff and customers never work around us.

The steps are set out in full on [how we work](/our-process/).

## Insured, covered and screened

Our crews are covered by WSIB Ontario and by liability insurance, the company is bonded, and staff are background-screened before they hold a key to anyone's building.

## Products

We clean with an approved line of professional [green cleaning products](/green-clean-products/), chosen so they do not leave residue in the air your staff breathe.

## Where we work

Toronto, including North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, East York and York, and [across the GTA](/locations/): Mississauga, Brampton, Oakville, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax and Oshawa. We have one office, downtown; crews travel to you.
