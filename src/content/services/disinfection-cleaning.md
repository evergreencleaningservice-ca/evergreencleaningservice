## Disinfection Cleaning for Toronto Workplaces

We can help to reduce the number of germs on surfaces by sanitizing them. We’ll do a thorough cleaning, as this physically removes dirt and germs. Disinfecting can get rid of germs on objects, as well as surfaces.

If you want to prevent bacteria and germs from spreading within your office, Evergreen Office Cleaning can help. We’ll perform a deep clean, while sanitizing your surfaces.

**_You’ll have peace of mind knowing your office will be as clean as possible._**

![Cleaner spraying and wiping a glass office door handle](/images/door-handle-disinfection-office.jpg)

Colds, flu and other viruses spread through coughs, sneezes and close contact — and through the surfaces everyone touches: door handles, light switches, shared desks and kitchen counters.

### How We Disinfect an Office

  
Before we disinfect, we’ll need to clean all surfaces, objects and contents. If this is not done initially, then the disinfectant we use will not be able to penetrate grime and dirt. In turn, surfaces won’t be thoroughly disinfected.

![Cleaner in protective gloves disinfecting a commercial workspace](/images/commercial-disinfection-service.jpg)

**_Quite simply, everything is cleaned with detergent first to remove surface residue, and then disinfected. We use specialized products for disinfection cleaning._**

### Products You Should Use for Disinfecting an Office

  
Many products on the market contain harsh chemicals, which is why they can decontaminate surfaces. However, these chemicals will usually linger within the office, which means the air quality will suffer and your employees' well-being will be affected, too. This is where the selection of cleaning products and how they’re used plays an important role in disinfection cleaning.
