## What emergency cleaning is

Emergency commercial cleaning is a clean-up booked because something has already happened: water on the floor, a space left in a state after an event, or a tenant arriving before the unit is ready. Evergreen Office Cleaning has cleaned commercial property in Toronto and the GTA since 1989, and the same crews that clean offices and buildings on a schedule take on these jobs when they come up.

![Air movers drying wet carpet in a corridor](/images/flooded-office-water-extraction.jpg)

The first thing we do is listen: what happened, which rooms, what the floors are, and when the space has to be usable again. That tells us what to bring and how many people to send, and it lets us tell you honestly whether the job is cleaning or something a restoration contractor should handle first.
