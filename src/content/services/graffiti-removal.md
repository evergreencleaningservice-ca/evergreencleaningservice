## Graffiti costs a property more the longer it stays up.

A tagged wall attracts more tagging, and in Toronto a property standing in violation of the municipal graffiti by-law can be ordered to remove it at its own expense. The answer is speed — and a method chosen for the surface, because the wrong solvent on the wrong substrate leaves a shadow worse than the tag.

Evergreen Office Cleaning removes graffiti from commercial property across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

## We remove graffiti from:

-   Masonry, brick and precast concrete
-   Painted and coated metal
-   Roll shutters and service doors
-   Signage and storefront glass
-   Loading areas and refuse enclosures

## How it is handled

We look at the surface before we touch it. Porous masonry, powder-coated metal and glass each take a different approach — chemical removal, pressure, or a combination — and some surfaces need repainting rather than cleaning, which we will tell you before starting rather than after.

For properties that get tagged repeatedly, we can quote a standing arrangement with an agreed response time rather than pricing each incident.
