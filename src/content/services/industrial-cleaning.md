## What industrial cleaning covers

Industrial cleaning is the cleaning of plants, factories, warehouses and other industrial buildings: the floors and high surfaces of the working areas, and the staff facilities and offices attached to them. Industrial spaces collect dust, dirt and clutter quickly, and with little natural light that makes them less safe and less pleasant to work in.

![Floor scrubber in a warehouse aisle](/images/warehouse-floor-scrubber-industrial.jpg)

A clean, regularly maintained facility helps worker safety, and it tells customers and partners that the operation is run well. Whether the site is large or small, the cleaning is set to what it needs, and equipment is worked around with care.
