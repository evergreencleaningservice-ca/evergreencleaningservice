## A finished build is not a finished space until the dust is gone.

Construction dust settles for days after the trades leave. It works into carpet, sits on ledges and light fixtures, and comes back twice more after the first clean. Getting a commercial space ready for handover takes more than a sweep — it takes a sequence.

Evergreen Office Cleaning has been cleaning commercial buildouts in Toronto since 1989, for general contractors, property managers and tenants taking possession.

## The three stages

-   **Rough clean** — debris removal, sweeping and the first pass, run while the trades are still finishing so the site stays workable.
-   **Secondary clean** — surfaces, fixtures, interior glass and washrooms, once the last of the dust has settled out of the air.
-   **Final turnover clean** — the detail pass: frames, tracks, vents, light fixtures, floor finish and glass, to the standard the space will be handed over at.

## We turn over:

-   Office buildouts and tenant improvements
-   Retail units and showrooms
-   Medical and dental fit-outs
-   Industrial and warehouse space
-   Common areas and lobbies in renovated buildings
