## Pressure washing is the difference between a building that looks maintained and one that looks neglected.

Exterior surfaces collect what the weather leaves behind — road salt through the winter, pollen and dust through the spring, grease and traffic film year round. None of it comes off with a broom, and on a commercial property it is the first thing a visitor sees.

Evergreen Office Cleaning provides pressure washing across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area, scheduled around your operating hours so that walkways, entrances and loading areas are clear when you need them.

## We pressure wash:

-   Building exteriors and façades
-   Entrances, walkways and sidewalks
-   Loading docks and service yards
-   Parking structures and surface lots
-   Refuse and recycling enclosures
-   Patios, terraces and outdoor seating areas

## How the work is scoped

Every property is quoted after an on-site walkthrough. Surface type, access, water supply and drainage all change the method — a precast façade and a concrete loading dock are not cleaned the same way, and using the wrong pressure on the wrong surface does damage that costs more than the clean.

Work is scheduled after hours or at weekends wherever the area is in use during the day.
