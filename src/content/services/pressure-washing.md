## What commercial pressure washing is

Commercial pressure washing is cleaning a business property's exterior surfaces (façades, walkways, entrances, docks and parking areas) with pressurized water, set to what each surface can take.

## The difference between maintained and neglected

Exterior surfaces collect what the weather leaves behind — road salt through the winter, pollen and dust through the spring, grease and traffic film year round. None of it comes off with a broom, and on a commercial property it is the first thing a visitor sees.
